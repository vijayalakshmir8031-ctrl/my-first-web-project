ABOUT US

Welcome to MAHALAXMI INDUSTRIAL ASSOCIATION

Mahalaxmi Industrial Association was established in 1993 under the Karnataka Societies Act. The association has been working continuously for the development of industries, trade and commerce by supporting entrepreneurs, promoting skill development and encouraging industrial growth.

Our association represents industries from textile & garments, agriculture, automobiles and heavy industries while working closely with government organizations and business leaders for sustainable industrial development.

Read More

Our Vision

To become a nationally reputed industrial association promoting scientific knowledge, technological innovation, professional ethics, and sustainable industrial growth across textile, agriculture, automobile and heavy industries.

Our Mission

To organize skill development programs, seminars, conferences, industrial exhibitions and professional training that improve industrial quality, productivity and entrepreneurship.

WHAT WE DO

Our Aims & Objectives

Committed to strengthening industries, businesses, and entrepreneurs through innovation, education, and collaboration.

Industrial Development

Promote the growth of industries, trade, and commerce by encouraging collaboration and innovation.

Skill Development

Conduct workshops, training programs, seminars, and conferences for entrepreneurs and professionals.

Knowledge Sharing

Publish books, newsletters, bulletins, and technical information for industrial development.

Government Support

Represent industries before local, state, and central government authorities.

Global Networking

Build relationships with trade organizations, chambers of commerce, and international institutions.

Business Growth

Support entrepreneurship and improve productivity through modern business practices.
